Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: November 24, 2026
Android package: com.simplylauncher.app
Contact: support@simplylauncher.app

1. Summary

Simply Launcher is a distraction-free Android home screen and optional digital-wellbeing toolkit. Most information stays on your device. The app does not create a Simply Launcher cloud account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include analytics SDKs in the current build.

Some features process sensitive device data on device (installed apps, usage stats, notifications, accessibility window titles) or send limited network requests for weather. Those features are optional or inherent to launcher functionality and are described below.

Viewing this Privacy Policy is informational. Continuing past onboarding is not treated as consent to optional sensitive data access. Runtime permissions and system toggles are requested when you enable the related feature.

2. Who we are

This policy applies to the Simply Launcher Android application published under package name com.simplylauncher.app.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@simplylauncher.app.

3. Information the app accesses or processes

3.1 On-device preferences (local storage)

Using Android Jetpack DataStore / local preferences, the app stores settings you configure, for example:

  • Favorites, folders, renamed labels, hidden apps, recently used apps
  • Theme, font, widget, and gesture preferences
  • Focus / blocking schedules and related task metadata
  • Notification filter rules and wellbeing feature toggles
  • Onboarding completion state

This data remains on your device. The current build does not sync it to Simply Launcher servers.

3.2 Installed / launchable apps

As a home-screen launcher, the app reads launchable activities through Android LauncherApps (and related package queries) so you can browse and open apps, including work-profile apps when available. App inventory is processed on device to power the home screen and drawer and is not uploaded to Simply Launcher servers.

3.3 Location and weather (optional)

If the weather widget is enabled, the app may:

  • Request approximate or precise location permission, and/or
  • Use network-based locality (for example GeoJS IP locality) as a fallback
  • Send latitude/longitude (or equivalent locality) to weather providers to fetch conditions

Network weather sources used by the app include:

  • Open-Meteo forecast API (api.open-meteo.com)
  • Open-Meteo air-quality API (air-quality-api.open-meteo.com)
  • GeoJS IP geo lookup (get.geojs.io) as a locality fallback
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to resolve a city name

The app may also read weather-related values exposed by OEM/system weather ContentProviders when present. Location is used to fulfill weather/locality display and is not sold by Simply Launcher.

3.4 Usage access (optional)

If you enable features such as app blocking or in-app time reminders, Android Usage Access may be required so the app can detect foreground apps and enforce limits. Usage information is processed on device for that feature.

3.5 Display over other apps (optional)

Overlay permission may be requested for time reminders, mindful launch delay, blocking overlays, or Focus task countdown. Overlay content is generated locally and is not uploaded to Simply Launcher servers.

3.6 Notification access (optional)

If you enable Notification Filter, a Notification Listener Service may read notifications to apply your filter rules. Notification content is processed on device for filtering and is not uploaded to Simply Launcher servers.

3.7 Accessibility (optional)

Accessibility may be requested for double-tap to lock and optional short-video exit helpers. The service may inspect window titles for short-video detection. Window titles are not stored as a history by Simply Launcher.

3.8 Alarms and notifications (optional / Focus)

Focus / today’s task reminders may schedule exact alarms (user-granted where required) and post notifications. Alarm metadata stays on device (and may interact with the system Clock app / companion Dayline Planner when installed).

3.9 Dayline Planner integration (optional)

If the companion Dayline Planner app is installed, Simply Launcher may exchange schedule/task information with it via declared ContentProvider / broadcast integrations so Focus tasks stay in sync. This stays between apps on your device.

4. How information is used

  • Provide launcher home/drawer functionality
  • Remember your preferences and organization
  • Power optional wellbeing features you enable
  • Show weather/locality on the home widget when enabled
  • Respond to support requests you email us

We do not sell personal information and do not use advertising SDKs in this app.

5. Sharing

Simply Launcher does not operate a backend that receives your preferences or app inventory. Limited data may be transmitted to third-party service providers solely to return weather/locality results (Open-Meteo, GeoJS, Nominatim) when those features run. Those providers process requests under their own terms/policies.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety, to the limited extent we hold it (for example, email correspondence you send us).

6. SDKs and libraries

The current release uses standard AndroidX / Jetpack libraries (Compose, Navigation, Lifecycle, DataStore, etc.). It does not include Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, Facebook SDK, or similar advertising / analytics SDKs.

7. Security

Local preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Weather network requests use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please keep your device locked and updated.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data is retained until you change it, clear app storage, or uninstall Simply Launcher. Clearing storage / uninstalling deletes local preferences.

Emails you send to support may be retained as needed to respond and for ordinary business records. To request deletion of support correspondence, email support@simplylauncher.app.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Disable weather / revoke location permission in system settings
  • Disable wellbeing features and revoke Usage Access, Overlay, Notification access, or Accessibility
  • Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data
  • Contact us for privacy questions or requests where applicable law provides additional rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR/UK GDPR or certain U.S. state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal data. Because most processing is local to your device, the most effective deletion method is clearing app data or uninstalling. Contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Children

Simply Launcher is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or legal requirements change. The “last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes may also be noted in an app update or store listing.
