Simply Launcher

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Simply Launcher for Android (com.simplylauncher.app). You can use the launcher without an account. The app uses AndroidX libraries including Compose, Navigation, Lifecycle and DataStore. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

For privacy questions or requests, email support@simplylauncher.app.

Information used on your device

  • Apps and launcher settings. Android provides the launchable apps, labels and icons that populate your home screen and app drawer, including work-profile apps when available. The launcher saves your favorites, folders, custom labels, hidden apps, recent launches, appearance settings, and feature preferences in private app storage.
  • Usage access. If you grant Android Usage Access, app identifiers, foreground activity and usage times can be read while you use other apps. The launcher processes this information locally to support screen-time reminders and app limits. Display-over-other-apps access shows reminders, countdowns and blocking screens.
  • Notification Filter. With notification access and filtering enabled, the launcher dismisses eligible notifications from apps you select and saves their app identifiers, app labels, titles, message text and timestamps in a local inbox. The inbox holds up to 100 entries and discards the oldest entries as new ones arrive. You can delete individual entries or clear the inbox. Turning off the filter stops new entries. Existing entries stay until removed.
  • Accessibility. If you enable the Android accessibility service, it can lock the screen, open the notification shade or interact with Android's color-correction controls. With short-video blocking enabled, it checks supported apps' package and class names, interface text, content descriptions and view identifiers to detect short videos and navigate back. This inspection is performed on the device. The launcher does not keep a history of inspected screen content or send it off the device.
  • Focus tasks and alarms. The launcher stores your focus schedules and task details locally. It can set alarms, show task countdowns and post notifications. Task labels and alarm times can be passed to the device's Clock app. When Dayline Planner is installed, Simply Launcher can automatically exchange schedule and task data with it, including task names, dates, times, priority, completion state and associated app identifiers. That transfer happens between apps on the device.

Online weather and location

The Weather widget asks you to enable online weather before it requests location or contacts weather services. It can use Android's last known location when permission is granted and may read weather data from a system weather provider. If a device location is unavailable or you decline the Android location permission, GeoJS can estimate your area from your public IP address.

  • Open-Meteo receives latitude and longitude for weather forecasts, UV and air quality.
  • GeoJS receives your IP address when the app needs to estimate your area.
  • Android's geocoder and OpenStreetMap Nominatim can receive coordinates to resolve a city name. The launcher tries Android's geocoder and can use Nominatim as a fallback.

Direct weather requests use HTTPS and expose your IP address and ordinary request metadata to their recipients. Providers handle requests under their own policies and may retain data independently. The app caches the latest weather result in memory. You can stop future requests by turning off Weather in widget settings. Revoking Android location access alone can still leave IP-based weather active if you enabled online weather.

If you open a weather app or browser from the widget, that service handles its own data. GitHub Pages hosts this policy and receives standard web request information, such as your IP address, when you open the page. The policy page uses no forms, analytics scripts or cookies of its own.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to reply and handle your request. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the issue, keep necessary records and meet legal obligations. We may disclose information we hold when legally required.

Security, retention and deletion

Private app storage is protected by Android's app sandbox. Launcher and app-gate DataStore files, including the filtered notification inbox, are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer under the app's backup rules. Android may back up other eligible app data, such as alarm metadata, depending on your device and backup settings.

Launcher settings and local records stay on your device until you update or remove them, clear Simply Launcher's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. You can manage any copies held by Clock, Dayline Planner or Android backup using their respective settings. Clearing Simply Launcher's storage does not erase a provider's records or email you have sent.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can turn off optional features in the app and revoke system access in Android settings, including location, Usage Access, notification access, display over other apps and Accessibility. Opening the Privacy Policy and continuing through onboarding are independent of your choices about optional features.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, or restrict or object to processing of personal information we hold. You may also have the right to complain to your local privacy regulator. To exercise a right or ask about support correspondence, contact support@simplylauncher.app.

Children and policy updates

Simply Launcher is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

We will post updates on this page and revise the effective date when this policy changes.
